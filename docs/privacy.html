Deadlock Counter Advisor

Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026

Deadlock Counter Advisor (“the app”) is an independent, unofficial second-screen companion for Valve’s game Deadlock. It suggests which items to buy against the enemy team. This policy covers both editions of the app: the standard edition and the Overwolf edition, which runs on Overwolf’s platform and receives live match data from it.

In short

  • The app has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics of its own. It does not sell or share your data.
  • What you enter and what the app saves stay on your PC.
  • The app connects to a few public services only for the features listed below. In the Overwolf edition, Overwolf’s platform handles its own data under Overwolf’s privacy policy.

Data that stays on your PC

  • Settings and layout: text size, window size and position, which panels are open, whether you have seen the intro.
  • Enemy lineup: the enemy heroes and items in the app, kept between launches. Your own hero, souls and items start fresh on every launch.
  • Catalog cache: the public item and hero list from your last sync.
  • Build data cache: which items appear in popular builds and item win rates for heroes you have played recently. Build names, descriptions and authors are not kept.
  • Post-match debrief: the last match ID you entered and, if you choose to enter it, your Steam friend code. The friend code is only used on your PC to find which player you were; it is never sent anywhere.

This data is stored in the app’s own storage under your Windows user profile. Uninstalling the app, or deleting its folder in %APPDATA%, removes it.

Live match data (Overwolf edition)

When a Deadlock match runs, Overwolf’s game events service gives the app the match roster (in-game player names, heroes, teams, souls), the items each player has bought, and the match ID, clock and phase. This is information the in-game scoreboard already shows.

  • The app uses this data on your PC, in memory, to fill in the enemy lineup, your hero, souls and items, and to see which heroes and items your teammates have so the picks fit your team. Teammates’ heroes and items are not saved.
  • In-game player names are used only to match items to players. They are not saved.
  • The app does not send this data anywhere, and it does not use or store Steam IDs.

The app never reads the game’s memory, modifies game files or sends input to the game.

Internet connections the app makes

Like any internet connection, each of these services can see your IP address. Each has its own privacy policy.

  • Catalog sync (when you click Sync, or before your first debrief): public item and hero data and artwork from deadlock.io.
  • Post-match debrief (when you load a match): the public match record for the match ID you entered, from the community service deadlock-api.com. Other players’ account IDs in that record are removed on your PC before display and are not stored.
  • Build data (on by default; turn it off under Settings → Privacy): once your hero is known, the app asks deadlock-api.com for popular community builds and item statistics for that hero and, while laning, your lane opponents’ heroes. Only hero IDs are sent. The results are kept on your PC for 12 hours.
  • Update checks (standard edition, at launch): the app asks GitHub Releases whether a newer version exists. Updates download only when you choose to.
  • Overwolf platform (Overwolf edition): Overwolf’s runtime connects to Overwolf to verify the app, provide the game events service and manage consent. Overwolf may collect usage data as described in the Overwolf Platform Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Every feature that uses the internet is optional except the Overwolf platform connection in the Overwolf edition. Recommendations work offline.
  • To stop build data requests, turn off Use community builds and match stats under Settings → Privacy.
  • In the Overwolf edition, open Settings → Privacy → Manage to review or change your consent choices for Overwolf’s platform at any time.
  • You can delete everything the app stores by uninstalling it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.
