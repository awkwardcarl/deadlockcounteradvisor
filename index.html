About & safety
Independent second-screen app

Manual recommendations always work. The Overwolf edition fills in the enemy lineup and their items, your hero, souls and purchases live from Overwolf's approved game events. The standard edition can watch for the game process. This app does not read Deadlock memory or inject an overlay.

How rankings work

Counters are explainable picks based on the enemy lineup, their visible items, your hero and your souls. Enemy heroes and recognized items contribute threat scores, and each counter is ranked by relevant protections and synergy. Suggestions are heuristics, not measured win-rate predictions.

Game-safety boundary

Live data comes only from Overwolf's approved game events: information the in-game scoreboard already shows. No memory reading, game injection, overlay or input automation, and the app sends no usage data of its own. The standard edition checks process names and can read an event file you choose, for testing.

Welcome to Counter Advisor

  1. Know what to buy next. The top of the window shows three next-item picks against the enemy team, with more options and the reasons below.
  2. Live match data. Start a Deadlock match and the enemy heroes and their items, your hero, souls and purchases fill in by themselves. You can still change anything.
  3. Enter the match. Pick the enemy heroes and add the items you see them buy. Your souls and items sharpen the picks.
  4. Review after the match. Post-match debrief compares what you bought with what would have countered the enemy team.
  5. Keep it on your second screen. Press Ctrl+Shift+F12 in game to show or hide this window. Use ↗ Move display to send it to another screen.
